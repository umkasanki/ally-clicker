CC0 is a public-domain dedication: these three may be copied, modified and
redistributed, commercially included, with no attribution required. They are
credited here anyway, because a repository that ships someone else's files should
be able to say where they came from.

Taken from Freesound's public MP3 preview — the original WAVs are behind a login —
then converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono WAV and trimmed. Each carried leading
silence from the MP3 encoder (24, 39 and 4 ms), which in a click is heard as the
sound lagging behind the press, and a long silent tail. Trimmed to 121, 86 and
46 ms, next to the 100 ms of the cue Apple's own Dwell Control uses for this job.

`Tap.wav` and `Tock.wav` predate this file and their origin was never written
down. This repository is public, so those two are being redistributed on terms
nobody can currently state. If they cannot be traced, replace them with CC0
equivalents rather than keep shipping them.
