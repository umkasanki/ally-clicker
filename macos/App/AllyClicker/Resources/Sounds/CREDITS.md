Each licence was read from the sound's own page rather than trusted to a search
filter. Freesound serves originals only to logged-in users, so these come from the
public MP3 preview, converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono WAV and trimmed: previews
carry leading silence from the encoder, which in a click is heard as the sound
lagging behind the press, and long silent tails.

Levels are left as the authors made them, by the user's decision — peaks run from
0.06 (Soft) to 0.98 (Thud), so the volume slider means different loudnesses for
different choices. Normalising them was offered and declined.

**The earlier Tap.wav and Tock.wav are gone.** They had been here since July with
no record of where they came from, and this repository is public, so they were
being redistributed on terms nobody could state. The names are kept — settings
store the name, not the file — and now carry CC0 sounds of the same character:
29 ms against the old 30, and 63 ms against the old 55.
